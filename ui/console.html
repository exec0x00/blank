Session keys are encrypted with the Space password and saved in the private bucket, so accounts reconnect after a restart.

Compaction & handoff

          
History is summarized in chunks of at most 12,000 estimated tokens. A compaction item keeps up to four recent text messages within 8,000 tokens. With handoff="compact", a linked turn can move to another account before Claude accepts it. Both consume usage allowance.
