2Layouts

2.1Straight line

A procedure with no choices. Read top to bottom, in the order you would write it in the exam. Used in F6, F10, F13, F14.

2.2Fork and rejoin

One question chooses the formula; both routes come back to the same final step. Used in F9, F11.

2.3Pick a route

One question with several answers. The grey tag on the right is the keyword to remember for each route. Used in F2.

2.4Two streams meet

Work each column on its own, then combine them. Most numericals follow this shape. Used in F4, F5, F8.

2.5Decision ladder

Ask the questions in order and stop at the first “yes”; that row is your answer. Used in F3, F7.

2.6Family tree

A topic split into branches, each listing its members in a small table. Use it for “classify” and “compare” answers. Used in F1, F12.

2.7Decision ladder for maths

The same ladder used to pick a differentiation rule in Unit 1 of UE26MA141A. The tag on each answer lists the matching assignment problems.

2.8Repeat until

A cycle that runs until a condition is met. The return line on the right goes back to the step that repeats. Used in F10.

2.9Verify both sides

For “verify” and “show that” questions. Work out each side separately, then compare them in a check block.
