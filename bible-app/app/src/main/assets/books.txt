1	GEN	Genesis	Gen	OT	The book of beginnings: creation, the first families, and the stories of Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph.
2	EXO	Exodus	Exod	OT	How God frees Israel from slavery in Egypt, gives the law at Sinai and comes to live among his people.
3	LEV	Leviticus	Lev	OT	Instructions for worship, offerings and holy living, given to Israel at Sinai.
4	NUM	Numbers	Num	OT	Israel's forty years in the wilderness, from the census at Sinai to the edge of the promised land.
5	DEU	Deuteronomy	Deut	OT	Moses' farewell speeches, calling Israel to remember the covenant before entering the land.
6	JOS	Joshua	Josh	OT	Joshua leads Israel into the promised land and divides it among the tribes.
7	JDG	Judges	Judg	OT	A cycle of failure and rescue as Israel is led by judges like Deborah, Gideon and Samson.
8	RUT	Ruth	Ruth	OT	A short story of loyalty and redemption, set in the days of the judges.
9	1SA	1 Samuel	1Sam	OT	The prophet Samuel anoints Israel's first kings, Saul and then David.
10	2SA	2 Samuel	2Sam	OT	David's reign as king of Israel, with its triumphs and its failures.
11	1KI	1 Kings	1Kgs	OT	Solomon builds the temple, then the kingdom divides and Elijah confronts its kings.
12	2KI	2 Kings	2Kgs	OT	The kings of Israel and Judah decline until both kingdoms are taken into exile.
13	1CH	1 Chronicles	1Chr	OT	A retelling of Israel's story from Adam to David, centred on the temple and worship.
14	2CH	2 Chronicles	2Chr	OT	From Solomon's temple to the exile and the call to return, told through the kings of Judah.
15	EZR	Ezra	Ezra	OT	The exiles return from Babylon to rebuild the temple and renew the law.
16	NEH	Nehemiah	Neh	OT	Nehemiah rebuilds the walls of Jerusalem and leads the people in renewing the covenant.
17	EST	Esther	Esth	OT	A Jewish queen in Persia risks her life to save her people.
18	JOB	Job	Job	OT	A righteous man suffers terribly, and he and his friends wrestle with why.
19	PSA	Psalms	Ps	OT	Israel's songbook of 150 prayers and praises: lament, thanks, trust and worship.
20	PRO	Proverbs	Prov	OT	Short sayings of wisdom about how to live well.
21	ECC	Ecclesiastes	Eccl	OT	A teacher reflects on work, pleasure and death, and asks what makes life meaningful.
22	SNG	Song of Songs	Song	OT	A series of love poems celebrating devotion between a bride and groom.
23	ISA	Isaiah	Isa	OT	The prophet Isaiah speaks of judgment and comfort, and of a coming servant and king.
24	JER	Jeremiah	Jer	OT	Jeremiah warns Judah of coming exile and promises a new covenant.
25	LAM	Lamentations	Lam	OT	Five poems of grief over the fall of Jerusalem.
26	EZK	Ezekiel	Ezek	OT	Ezekiel's visions among the exiles in Babylon: judgment first, then hope of restoration.
27	DAN	Daniel	Dan	OT	Stories of faithfulness in exile in Babylon, and visions of the future.
28	HOS	Hosea	Hos	OT	Hosea's own marriage pictures God's faithful love for an unfaithful Israel.
29	JOL	Joel	Joel	OT	A locust plague becomes a call to return to God, with a promise of his Spirit.
30	AMO	Amos	Amos	OT	A shepherd-prophet calls Israel to justice and honest worship.
31	OBA	Obadiah	Obad	OT	A short prophecy against Edom for its pride and cruelty.
32	JON	Jonah	Jonah	OT	A reluctant prophet is sent to Nineveh and learns about God's mercy.
33	MIC	Micah	Mic	OT	Micah calls for justice and mercy and promises a ruler from Bethlehem.
34	NAM	Nahum	Nah	OT	A prophecy about the fall of Nineveh.
35	HAB	Habakkuk	Hab	OT	Habakkuk questions God about injustice and learns to trust him.
36	ZEP	Zephaniah	Zeph	OT	A warning of the day of the LORD, with a promise to restore a humble remnant.
37	HAG	Haggai	Hag	OT	Haggai urges the returned exiles to finish rebuilding the temple.
38	ZEC	Zechariah	Zech	OT	Visions and promises that encourage the rebuilding of the temple and look to a coming king.
39	MAL	Malachi	Mal	OT	The last prophet of the Old Testament calls the people back to faithful worship.
40	MAT	Matthew	Matt	NT	The life and teaching of Jesus, written to show him as Israel's promised king.
41	MRK	Mark	Mark	NT	The shortest and fastest-moving account of Jesus' life and ministry.
42	LUK	Luke	Luke	NT	Luke's orderly account of Jesus' life, written so a reader could be sure of what they had been taught.
43	JHN	John	John	NT	An account of Jesus' life written so that readers will believe and have life.
44	ACT	Acts	Acts	NT	How the good news spreads from Jerusalem across the Roman world.
45	ROM	Romans	Rom	NT	Paul explains the gospel and how it changes the way we live.
46	1CO	1 Corinthians	1Cor	NT	Paul answers questions and problems in the divided church in Corinth.
47	2CO	2 Corinthians	2Cor	NT	Paul defends his ministry and writes about weakness, comfort and generosity.
48	GAL	Galatians	Gal	NT	Paul insists that we are made right with God by faith, not by keeping the law.
49	EPH	Ephesians	Eph	NT	Paul describes the church as one family in Christ, and how to live as one.
50	PHP	Philippians	Phil	NT	A warm letter of joy, written by Paul from prison.
51	COL	Colossians	Col	NT	Paul shows that Christ is supreme over everything.
52	1TH	1 Thessalonians	1Th	NT	Paul encourages a young church to stand firm and hope in Christ's return.
53	2TH	2 Thessalonians	2Th	NT	Paul corrects confusion about Christ's return and urges steady work.
54	1TI	1 Timothy	1Tim	NT	Paul advises Timothy on leading and caring for the church.
55	2TI	2 Timothy	2Tim	NT	Paul's last letter, urging Timothy to stay faithful.
56	TIT	Titus	Titus	NT	Paul tells Titus how to order the church in Crete and teach good living.
57	PHM	Philemon	Phlm	NT	A personal appeal to welcome back a runaway slave as a brother.
58	HEB	Hebrews	Heb	NT	Shows that Jesus is greater than the prophets, the angels and the old priesthood.
59	JAS	James	Jas	NT	Practical advice on living out faith through action.
60	1PE	1 Peter	1Pet	NT	Peter encourages Christians who are suffering for their faith.
61	2PE	2 Peter	2Pet	NT	Peter warns against false teachers and urges growth in faith.
62	1JN	1 John	1Jn	NT	John writes about love, truth and knowing that we have eternal life.
63	2JN	2 John	2Jn	NT	A short letter urging truth and love, and caution about false teachers.
64	3JN	3 John	3Jn	NT	A short letter praising hospitality to traveling teachers.
65	JUD	Jude	Jude	NT	A short warning to defend the faith against false teachers.
66	REV	Revelation	Rev	NT	John's visions of Christ's victory and of a new heaven and a new earth.
