1Short notes

1 · Implicit functions, level surfaces and the differential
A relation f(x,y)=c or f(x1​,…,xn​)=c defines an implicit function; geometrically it is a level curve or level surface (isotherm, equipotential surface), e.g. the cylinders x2+y2=c or spheres x2+y2+z2=c. On it f does not change, so its total differential df=fx​dx+fy​dy+⋯=0. The differential is also used in substitution in integrals and in error estimates.
2 · Implicit differentiation in two variables
An implicit function is given by a relation f(x,y)=c, a level curve. Along it df=fx​dx+fy​dy=0, so dxdy​=−fy​fx​​ (fy​=0). Direct differentiation of both sides gives the same result. The slope at (x0​,y0​) gives the tangent y−y0​=m(x−x0​); e.g. xey+yex=0 has tangent x+y=0 at the origin.
3 · Implicit functions of three variables
For f(x,y,z)=0, z is a function of x and y: ∂x∂z​=−fz​fx​​ (hold y), ∂y∂z​=−fz​fy​​, and ∂x∂y​=−fy​fx​​ (hold z), provided the denominators are non-zero. Check that the point lies on the surface. For second derivatives, remember that z still depends on x,y.
4 · Composite functions and the chain rule
If u=f(x,y) and x,y depend on s,t (or r,θ), u is a composite function of s,t, and us​=fx​xs​+fy​ys​, ut​=fx​xt​+fy​yt​: one term per intermediate variable. Write the chain first. If f is unknown, keep fx​,fy​ as symbols. Eliminate x,y from the final answer. Standard results: ux​+uy​+uz​=0 for f(x−y,y−z,z−x); xux​+yuy​+zuz​=0 for f(x/y,y/z,z/x).
5 · Composite functions in polar coordinates
With x=rcosθ, y=rsinθ: xθ​=−rsinθ, yθ​=rcosθ; with r=x2+y2​, θ=tan−1(y/x): rx​=x/r, ry​=y/r, θx​=−y/r2, θy​=x/r2. Functions of x2+y2 alone have uθ​=0, tan−1(y/x) has uθ​=1, and ux2​+uy2​=ur2​+uθ2​/r2.

4Last 10 minutes before the exam

Top facts

  1. A relation f=c is a level curve or surface; on it df=fx​dx+fy​dy+⋯=0.
  2. Two variables: dy/dx=−fx​/fy​ (fy​=0). Slide 004·4 prints it upside down.
  3. Both routes (differentiate directly, or the formula) give the same dy/dx.
  4. Tangent at (x0​,y0​): y−y0​=m(x−x0​) with m=dy/dx there.
  5. Three variables: zx​=−fx​/fz​ (hold y), zy​=−fy​/fz​, yx​=−fx​/fy​ (hold z).
  6. Check that the point lies on the surface: the lecture's (1,0,2) does not.
  7. Composite: write the chain, then us​=fx​xs​+fy​ys​; keep unknown fx​,fy​; eliminate x,y.
  8. ESA favourites: ux​+uy​+uz​=0; xux​+yuy​+zuz​=0; zx2​+zy2​ identities; 2yzx​−zy​=0.
  9. Polar: xθ​=−rsinθ, yθ​=rcosθ; rx​=x/r, θx​=−y/r2.
  10. Functions of x2+y2 alone give uθ​=0; tan−1(y/x) gives uθ​=1.
