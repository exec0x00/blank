1Short notes

1 · Taylor expansion about a point (a, b)
About (a,b): h=x−a, k=y−b; table of f,fx​,fy​,fxx​,fxy​,fyy​ at the point; f+{hfx​+kfy​}+2!1​{h2fxx​+2hkfxy​+k2fyy​}. excosy at (1,4π​): 2​e​[1+h−k+21​(h2−2hk−k2)]. sin(xy) at (1,2π​): 1−8π2​h2−2π​hk−21​k2. xy2+ycos(x−y) at (1,1): 2+h+3k+21​(−h2+6hk+k2).
2 · Maclaurin expansion about the origin
About (0,0): f+{xfx​+yfy​}+2!1​{…}+3!1​{…}. log(2x+y+1)=(2x+y)−2(2x+y)2​; ex−y=1+(x−y)+2(x−y)2​+6(x−y)3​; exlog(1+y)=y+xy−2y2​+2x2y​−2xy2​+3y3​. Check one-combination functions with the one-variable series.
3 · Approximate values from a Taylor polynomial
Expand about a nearby easy point and substitute small h, k with signs. tan−1(xy) at (0.9,−1.2) from (1,−1): −0.835 (first order), −0.823 (second); exact −0.824. tan−11.10.9​≈0.6854.

4Last 10 minutes before the exam

Top facts

  1. Method: general series → derivative table → values at the point → substitute → check.
  2. h=x−a, k=y−b; second order 2!1​{h2fxx​+2hkfxy​+k2fyy​}; third order 3!1​{h3fxxx​+3h2kfxxy​+3hk2fxyy​+k3fyyy​}.
  3. excosy at (1,4π​): 2​e​[1+h−k+21​(h2−2hk−k2)] (ESA May 2024).
  4. Functions of one combination (2x+y, x−y, x+2y): use the one-variable series.
  5. Slide errors: fyy​(1,1)=1 (not 3) for xy2+ycos(x−y); fxy​(0,0)=1 (not 0) for exlog(1+y).
  6. Approximation: expand about a nearby easy point; keep second order (−0.823 vs exact −0.824).
  7. ESA: sinxsiny about (4π​,4π​) (2nd and 3rd degree), excosy, log(1+x−y), eaxsinby, sin(x+2y).
