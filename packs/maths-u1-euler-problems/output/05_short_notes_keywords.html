1Short notes

1 · Testing homogeneity: the scaling test
Scaling test: u(λx,λy)=λnu(x,y) means degree n (positive, negative or zero). Slide examples: (x2+y2)2x4+2x2y2+y4​ degree 0; x2+y2​sin−1(y/x)​ degree −1; (x+y)3x2y+xy2​ degree 0; x−yex−ey​ not homogeneous.
2 · Verifying Euler's theorem directly
To verify: find n, differentiate, form xux​+yuy​, simplify to nu. For Z=x​+y​x+y​ (n=21​): xZx​+yZy​=2(x​+y​)2(x+y)(x​+y​)​=21​Z.
3 · When u itself is homogeneous: first and second order
u homogeneous of degree n: xux​+yuy​=nu, x2uxx​+2xyuxy​+y2uyy​=n(n−1)u, sum n2u. x2+y2x−y​: −u, 2u. ESA: 5u & 20u; 9u; 17π/2; ratios only give 0.
4 · When f(u) is homogeneous: the extension
If f(u) has degree n: g=nf/f′, second order g(g′−1). ex3y3/(x2+y2): 4ulogu. logx+yx2+y2​: 1, −1. sin−1x​+y​x+y​: 21​tanu, 21​tanu(21​sec2u−1). sin−1x8+y8+z8​x+2y+3z​: −3tanu. ESA: tan−1xyx4+y4​ gives sin2u, sin2u(1−4sin2u).

Top facts

  1. Scaling test: u(λx,λy)=λnu; degree may be positive, negative or zero.
  2. No single λn factors out ⇒ not homogeneous (x−yex−ey​).
  3. u homogeneous: nu, n(n−1)u, together n2u; parts of different degrees separately.
  4. x2+y2x−y​: n=−1, answers −u and +2u.
  5. f(u) homogeneous: g=nf/f′; second order g(g′−1).
  6. sin−1→ntanu; tan−1→2n​sin2u; log→n (second order −n); e(⋅)→nulogu.
  7. Slide 006·4: −3tanu; slide 006·12: 4ulogu; slide 006·14: 1,−1; slide 006·16: 21​tanu.
