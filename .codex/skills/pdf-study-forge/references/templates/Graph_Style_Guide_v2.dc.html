1Style rules for graphs and diagrams

Restyle a figure without changing its data: every curve, point, axis range, label and annotation stays. Only colour, line weight, spacing and type change. Figures are drawn large, one per row, so labels read at text size.

1.1Colour

Okabe–Ito palette: distinct for colour-blind readers and on white paper. Use colours in this order.

Blue #0072B2
Series 1: the main curve, ground state, absorption
Vermilion #D55E00
Series 2: comparison curve, excited state, emission, forces
Green #009E73
Series 3: fundamental transitions, “active” results
Purple #A0557F
Series 4: electronic transitions, second boundaries
Orange #E69F00
Highlights: beams, the vertical Franck–Condon arrow
Tints
Fills for atoms, lobes and regions: the series colour at about 10%

1.2Lines and neutrals

Data curve 2.4–2.6 px, solid
Model or approximation dashed (harmonic, excited state)
Limit or reference grey dashed (dissociation, 1 atm)
Guide #d5d8dd, 1 px
Axes and text ink #1a1a1a; muted notes #5f6368

Never rely on colour alone: every series also has a direct label in its own colour, and models are dashed.

1.3Type and size

  • Computer Modern Serif for every label, as in the course PDFs.
  • Variables italic (E, B, λ, re); units upright (cm−1, °C, atm).
  • Draw on a 700-unit-wide canvas with 12–15 unit text; display at full column width (about 1000 px), so text reads at 17–21 px.
  • Leave at least 16 units between any two labels; move a label and add a thin leader line rather than overlap.

1.4Layout

  • One figure per row, full width. Never shrink a figure to sit beside another.
  • Keep each figure’s own proportions; tall figures (phase diagrams, potential curves) stay tall.
  • Left and bottom axes only; ticks outward; keep the original ranges and tick values.
  • Multi-panel figures stack panels vertically when side-by-side would make them narrow.
  • Caption: “Figure Gn.” title, ESA stars, then ID and source tags.
  • Tables and remarks are not drawn inside the graphic. Use the Table and Remark blocks from the flowchart template, placed under the figure.
