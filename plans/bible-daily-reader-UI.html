Manna.

UI concept · not a working build

One passage a day. Never the same twice.

Manna picks a short reading at random from the parts of the Bible you haven’t read yet. It stays within the length you choose and always ends at the end of a sentence.

The whole Bible, drawn to scale. Each book is as wide as it is long. The gold marks are the 330 passages one sample reader has finished: scattered at random, never overlapping, 9.6% of the way through. Hover or tap a book to see its progress.

“…the people shall go out and gather a day’s portion every day…”
Exodus 16:4 · World English Bible

Scroll inside each phone. Drag the length slider in Settings and the other screens update with it. All Scripture shown is World English Bible text. Progress figures come from a simulated reader.

Reading

One reading a day, chosen when the day starts. It begins where an earlier reading left off or at the top of a chapter, and it ends at the end of a sentence, usually at a paragraph break.

Progress and settings

Progress is counted in words, so a short book and a long one each count for what they are. In Settings you set the length and move your progress to another device.

Widgets

A verse of the day on the home screen, chosen from about 366 well-loved verses. The app and every widget show the same verse on the same day, even offline.

Design mockup only. Nothing here is a working app. Full details are in the build plan that accompanies this file.
