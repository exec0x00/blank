UI concept · daily Bible reading app · not a working build

Manna.

One short passage a day, picked at random from what you haven’t read. Never repeated, never longer than you choose, and always ending at the end of a sentence.

This is the whole Bible, drawn to scale: each book is as wide as it is long. The gold flecks are the 297 passages a sample reader has finished, scattered at random and never overlapping. The reader is 9.6% of the way through. Hover or tap a book to see its progress.

Below are the main screens, in the order a reader meets them. Scroll inside each phone. In Settings, drag the length slider and every screen updates. Try a length under 244 words to see today’s reading flagged for rebuilding. All Scripture shown is real World English Bible text. Progress figures come from a simulated reader.

Design mockup only. This page shows what the app would look like and how it behaves; nothing here is a working app. Full details are in the build plan that accompanies this file.
