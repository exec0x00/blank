# PESU all-subject study materials

This repository branch contains the source inventory, reproducible production pipeline, and study materials for every video lesson in the PESU bucket. It covers 215 videos in five subjects and all 26 official units across six subjects. The bucket has source folders for 20 units; the other six are labelled source-gap/reference packs. Each video has its own PDF/HTML, and every official unit has a complete master PDF/HTML. The current corpus and coverage details are in [inventory/coverage.md](inventory/coverage.md); the full file-level inventory with hashes and transcript sidecars is [inventory/source-inventory.json](inventory/source-inventory.json).

The PDF Study Forge skill used to build the deliverables is installed at [`.codex/skills/pdf-study-forge/SKILL.md`](../.codex/skills/pdf-study-forge/SKILL.md). See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for scope and release requirements. Raw downloads and rendering caches stay local and out of Git; source-grounded datasets, canonical unit/video packs, and build reports are committed to `allsubjects` as checkpoints. The generated PDFs and standalone HTML files are collected with the datasets and provenance reports in the release ZIP.
