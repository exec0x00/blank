# PESU all-subject study materials

This repository branch contains the source inventory, reproducible production pipeline, and study materials for every video lesson in the PESU bucket. The 215 videos are organized across five subjects and the course materials are grouped by unit. The current corpus and coverage details are in [inventory/coverage.md](inventory/coverage.md); the full file-level inventory with hashes and transcript sidecars is [inventory/source-inventory.json](inventory/source-inventory.json).

The PDF Study Forge skill used to build the deliverables is installed at [`.codex/skills/pdf-study-forge/SKILL.md`](../.codex/skills/pdf-study-forge/SKILL.md). See [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) for scope and release requirements. Source downloads and rendering caches stay local and out of Git; generated study datasets and deliverables are committed to `allsubjects` as checkpoints.
