1Man Cabbage Goat Wolf Problem

Study focus: Man Cabbage Goat Wolf Problem. The notes explain the key terms, distinctions, and practical method used in this lesson. Read the cited transcript intervals for source context, then use the examples to check your understanding. EV0000010 · EV0000011

short-note recap

Model the river puzzle as a state-space search. The farmer, wolf, goat, and cabbage begin on one bank. The boat carries the farmer plus at most one passenger. A state records which bank each item occupies.

A state is unsafe if the farmer leaves the wolf alone with the goat, or the goat alone with the cabbage. A legal move changes the farmer's bank and optionally moves one item with him; reject any move that creates an unsafe state.

Search the remaining legal states until the goal is reached, and keep a record of visited states to avoid cycling. The key computational idea is to make constraints explicit, generate only valid successors, and verify that the final state satisfies the goal.
