In this session, we'll understand partial differential equations.

Key details

  • Introduction to partial differential equations.
  • An equation involving independent variable, dependent variable and derivatives of the dependent variable with respect to independent variable is called a differential equation.
  • In this case x is the independent variable, y is dependent variable and the derivative of y with respect to x is nothing but dy by dx.
  • Differential equations are classified into two based on the number of independent variables present in it.
  • The first one ordinary differential equations and partial differential equations.
  • In the case of ordinary differential equations, we see that the derivatives are with respect to only one independent variable.
  • Also, the unknown function depends on a single variable.
  • And X is independent variable.
  • In a similar way, in partial differential equations we see that the partial derivatives are with respect to two or more independent variables.
  • The unknown function depends on multiple variables.
  • In this case u is dependent variable and x and t are independent variables.
  • A partial differential equation is an equation that involves partial derivatives of a function with several independent variables.
  • As in the case of ordinary differential equations, we have only one independent variable.
  • In the case of partial differential equations, we have two or more independent variables.
  • Some of the examples of partial differential equations are one dimensional heat conduction equation, Laplask equation, and one dimensional wave equation.
  • As we have seen earlier, one of the applications of ordinary differential equations is modeling.
